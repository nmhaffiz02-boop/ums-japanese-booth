Learn your first Japanese characters

Tap any character to see its sound. Start with Hiragana, then switch to Katakana and explore the two basic Japanese writing systems.

Hiragana is commonly used for native Japanese words and grammatical endings.

Choose a character to practise its pronunciation.

Basic gojūon characters only. Sounds are shown in romaji to help beginners get started.
